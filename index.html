How to play

  1. Take turns to roll the dice. You need a 6 to bring a token out of base.
  2. Move a token the number you rolled, clockwise round the board and up your coloured path.
  3. Land on an opponent and they go back to base. You get an extra turn.
  4. A 6, a capture or a token reaching home all give you another roll.
  5. Careful: three 6s in a row and you lose your turn.
  6. Squares where each colour starts are safe. Nobody can be sent home there.
  7. You need the exact roll to reach home. First to get all four home wins.
